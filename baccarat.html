How to Play Baccarat

Objective

Bet on which hand — Player or Banker — will have a total closest to 9. You can also bet on a Tie.

Card Values

  • Cards 2-9: Face value
  • 10, Jack, Queen, King: Worth 0
  • Ace: Worth 1
  • If total exceeds 9, only the last digit counts (e.g., 15 = 5)

How to Bet

  1. Select a chip value by clicking on it
  2. Click on Banker, Player, or Tie betting spot
  3. Click multiple times to increase your bet
  4. Click "Deal Cards" when ready

Payouts

  • Banker: 1:1 (minus 5% commission)
  • Player: 1:1
  • Tie: 8:1

Drawing Rules

If either hand totals 8 or 9 (a "natural"), no more cards are drawn. Otherwise, a third card may be drawn automatically according to preset Baccarat rules.

Pattern Tracking

The Bead Plate shows the history of results. Red circles = Banker wins, Blue circles = Player wins, Gold circles = Tie.
